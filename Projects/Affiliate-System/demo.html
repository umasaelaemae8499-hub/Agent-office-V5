Getting around

For a Tokyo–Kyoto–Osaka route, the 7-day Japan Rail Pass pays for itself with a single round trip on the shinkansen. Order it before you fly — it ships to your home address.

Where to stay

In Tokyo we recommend basing yourself near Shinjuku or Ueno. Compare Tokyo hotels on Booking.com or check Kyoto ryokan on Agoda for the traditional experience.

Things to do
