# TikTok Template: Science-Explainer Format (Dr Ben Miles style)

> Note: the reference video (tiktok.com/@drbenmiles/video/7488623418192039190) could not be
> viewed directly. This template is modelled on Dr Ben Miles' typical format: a scientist
> explaining a real breakthrough or surprising fact, backed by a cited study.
> Replace every `{{PLACEHOLDER}}` with your own content.

---

## 1. Format breakdown (why it works)

| Element | What he does | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook (0–2s)** | Opens mid-sentence on the most surprising claim, e.g. "Scientists just…" / "This shouldn't be possible…" | Stops the scroll before the viewer can swipe |
| **Credibility (2–5s)** | Mentions the source: journal, university, or "a new study" | Authority leads to trust and shares |
| **Explanation (5–35s)** | Talking head plus fast B-roll / diagrams every 2–3s | Visual change holds attention; the face builds connection |
| **The "so what" (35–50s)** | Why it matters to the viewer's life or future | Turns a fact into something they'll care about |
| **Close (50–60s)** | Open question or opinion prompt: "Would you…?" | Drives comments, which boost reach |
| **Captions** | Bold, centred, 2–4 words at a time, key word highlighted | ~80% watch on mute; this boosts retention |
| **Pacing** | Jump cuts, no pauses, 140–170 wpm | Feels energetic and dense with value |
| **Length** | 45–75 seconds, 9:16 vertical | Long enough for depth, short enough to finish |

## 2. Script template (fill in)

```
[0–2s HOOK]        "{{SHOCKING_CLAIM_IN_ONE_SENTENCE}}"
[2–5s SOURCE]      "A new {{STUDY/REPORT}} from {{INSTITUTION/SOURCE}} found that…"
[5–15s SETUP]      "Here's the problem: {{CONTEXT / WHAT PEOPLE ASSUME}}."
[15–35s CORE]      "What they did was {{HOW IT WORKS}}. And the result? {{KEY NUMBER/RESULT}}."
[35–50s SO WHAT]   "Which means {{IMPACT ON VIEWER / WORLD}}."
[50–60s CTA]       "{{OPINION QUESTION}}? Let me know below."
```

**On-screen text overlay (first frame):** `{{3–6 WORD HEADLINE}}`
**Caption:** `{{ONE-LINE SUMMARY}} 🔬 #{{topic}} #science #learnontiktok #fyp`

## 4. Tool workflow

### A. Gemini (script and Veo B-roll)
1. **Script prompt** (paste into Gemini):
   ```
   Write a 60-second TikTok script about {{TOPIC}} in the style of a scientist explainer.
   Structure: 2s shocking hook, cite the source, explain simply, give one key number,
   explain why it matters, end with an opinion question. Max 160 words, short punchy sentences.
   ```
2. **Veo B-roll prompts** (vertical 9:16, 8s each):
   ```
   Cinematic vertical 9:16 shot of {{SCENE}}, shallow depth of field, soft natural light,
   slow push-in camera move, photorealistic, no text.
   ```
   ```
   Macro 9:16 shot of {{OBJECT/PROCESS}}, glowing scientific lighting, slow rotation,
   clean dark background, high detail.
   ```

### B. Canva AI (graphics, thumbnail, captions styling)
1. Create a **TikTok Video (1080×1920)** design.
2. Use **Magic Media** to generate a diagram: `simple clean infographic showing {{PROCESS}}, flat style`.
3. Make a **"study card"**: source name, year, and one-line finding on a white card with a drop shadow.
4. Make a **big-number slide**: `{{KEY NUMBER}}` in 200pt bold with a one-line label.
5. Make a **cover/thumbnail**: your face cut out (Background Remover) plus the 3–6 word headline.
6. Export each as MP4/PNG, then bring them into CapCut.

### C. CapCut (edit and assembly)
1. Record the talking-head lines (shots 1 and 7, plus the narration) on your phone in one take; use **Auto-cut / Remove silences**.
2. Lay the Veo clips and Canva graphics over the narration (2–3s per visual change).
3. **Auto Captions**, then style: bold sans font, white with black stroke, highlight the key word in yellow, centred in the lower third.
4. Add **zoom-in keyframes** (100% to 110%) on talking-head shots to fake camera movement.
5. Add a subtle background track at -25 dB plus a "whoosh" SFX on cuts.
6. Export 1080×1920, 30fps, then post with the caption and hashtags.
